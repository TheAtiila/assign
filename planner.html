Before booking

Check the practical details

  • Confirm opening days, tour times and entrance charges directly.
  • Allow more road time than a map estimate when travelling between regions.
  • Keep copies of identification and important contact numbers.
  • Use registered transport and follow local safety advice.
  • Carry water, sun protection and suitable footwear.
