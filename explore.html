Destinations and routes

Choose a route that fits your time

The routes below group nearby experiences and leave room for travel. Times and fees change, so confirm details with operators before setting out.

Two to three days

Coastal heritage route

Cape CoastElminaKakum

Begin with a guided visit to Cape Coast Castle or Elmina Castle. The history is difficult, and a guided tour gives the site the attention it deserves. Add Kakum National Park on a separate morning, then reserve time for the fishing communities and coastline.

  • Best for history, coast and short forest walks
  • Travel early to avoid rushing between sites
  • Wear comfortable shoes and carry water

Two days

Forest and waterfall route

AburiBoti FallsEastern Region

Use Aburi as an easy introduction to the cooler hills north of Accra. Continue to Boti Falls when conditions and access are suitable. A hired car or planned group trip is usually simpler than linking several rural stops by public transport in one day.

  • Best for gardens, walking and landscape views
  • Check rainfall and trail conditions
  • Keep footwear dry enough for uneven ground

Three days

Kumasi culture route

KumasiBonwireNtonso

Start with Manhyia Palace Museum for Asante history, then visit Kejetia Market with enough time and attention for the scale of the area. Day trips to Bonwire and Ntonso introduce kente weaving and adinkra printing through the people who practise them.

  • Best for museums, craft and market life
  • Ask before photographing workers or stalls
  • Carry smaller cash denominations for local purchases

Three to four days

Northern nature route

TamaleMoleLarabanga

Allow a full travel day to reach Mole National Park from Tamale and book park activities in advance. A visit to Larabanga should respect local guidance, dress expectations and any limits on access to religious spaces.

  • Best for wildlife, savannah and community visits
  • Plan around long road distances
  • Follow all park and guide instructions
